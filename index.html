The Legacy

Coach Lou Holtz

Lou Holtz coached Notre Dame from 1986 through 1996, and his 1988 team won the national championship. He led with a standard of discipline, accountability, and heart, and generations of fans still quote him.

These shirts are made for the people who carry that standard forward: alumni, students, families, and longtime fans who want something to wear on game day, at a reunion, or any day they want to salute Lou. They also make an easy gift for the fan who still talks about 1988.
